Calculate Wireless Signal Strength

This calculator uses the Friis transmission equation to estimate received wireless signal power over a range of distances.

Pr = Pt + Gt + Gr - FSPL

FSPL = 20 log10(dkm) + 20 log10(fMHz) + 32.44
